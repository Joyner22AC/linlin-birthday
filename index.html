给琳琳For Linlin

Maligayang kaarawan, Linlin!

琳琳，生日快乐！愿你新的一岁学业顺利，碰到难题时总能慢慢找到思路，付出的努力也能收获自己满意的结果。更希望你身体健康，吃得好、睡得香，忙的时候也能好好照顾自己。

Happy birthday, Linlin! I hope your studies go well this year, that you find your way through the tough problems, and that your hard work pays off in ways you’re proud of. Most of all, I wish you good health, good meals, restful nights, and time to take care of yourself when life gets busy.

很开心能和你做朋友。希望往后的许多年，我们还会把遇见的趣事第一时间讲给对方听，也还会一起庆祝很多个生日。愿我们的友谊一直走下去。

I’m so glad we’re friends. I hope that years from now we’ll still think of each other first when something funny happens, and that we’ll get to celebrate many more birthdays together. Here’s to a friendship that lasts.

乐乐来给你过生日啦！A note from Joy

琳琳！听说你以前扮演过我？太棒了，这次轮到我来给你庆生！先吹蜡烛、吃口生日面，什么时候想把这一刻收起来，再来找我。

Linlin! I heard you dressed up as me once. That’s amazing! Now it’s my turn to celebrate you. Make a wish, have some birthday noodles, and come find me whenever you’re ready to save this moment.

Pancit bihon菲律宾生日面 · Filipino birthday noodles

菲律宾的生日聚会上常有一盘 pancit。长长的面条寄托着长寿的祝愿。这盘特意留给你，琳琳。愿你新的一岁健健康康，也愿我们的友谊长长久久。

Pancit is often served at Filipino birthdays, where its long noodles are a wish for a long life. This plate is for you, Linlin. Wishing you good health and many more years of friendship.

11 月 14 日的记忆A memory from November 14

琳琳，想念今天的时候，再来看看这颗球吧。愿你学业顺利、身体健康，也愿我们往后还有好多生日可以一起过。

Linlin, whenever you want to revisit today, come back to this little orb. I wish you good health, all the best with your studies, and many more birthdays to celebrate together.
